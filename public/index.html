One video, from idea to what you learn from it

Twelve Claude skills cover the whole workflow. Seven of the steps are backed by tools that measure instead of guessing.

  1. ScriptFive hooks from 21 formulas, scored, then the spoken script with retention beats.
  2. PackageTitle and thumbnail text linted together: truncation, repetition, hype.
  3. EditDead air, fillers and retakes as an edit list with timecodes; chapters that render.
  4. You publishNothing goes live until you upload it. Every step ends with "ship it, or change it?"
  5. LearnRetention: hook leak, the exact cliffs, and what you said there. Then plan the next one.

The tools

Each runs from the command line, from a Claude skill, or over a local API. No account, no sign-up.

Hook score

Five properties of an opening line, weighted toward its weakest. A prompt to look again, not a forecast.

yt-hookscore

Title + thumbnail lint

Desktop and mobile truncation, shouting, vague words, repeated text, and claims you'd have to prove.

yt-title

Edit list

Dead air, filler and retakes with timecodes. Uses word timings when your transcript has them.

yt-deadair

Chapters

Drafted from your pauses and topic shifts, checked against YouTube's rules so they actually appear.

yt-chapters

Shorts finder

20–55 second moments that stand on their own, for you to re-hook and cut.

yt-shorts

Retention reader

Hook leak, cliffs and slide from your Studio export, with what you were saying at each drop.

yt-retention

Outlier research

Videos ranked by how far they beat their own channel's median, not by channel size.

What made viewers leave

Across all your videos: the kinds of sentences (sponsor reads, tangents, subscribe asks) that come right before people leave.

Pre-upload check

One command before you upload: opening, title and thumbnail, edit, description, chapters and search, ending in a clear go or fix.

What the numbers mean, and what they don't

We'd rather you trust the tools a little less and use them well.

Scores are heuristics

The hook and title scores come from word patterns. They don't predict views, and no tool here claims to.

Retention depends on context

Small view counts, video age, format and audience all change the curve. The report says so every time.

Edits need a listen

A transcript can't tell a dramatic pause from dead air. That's why short pauses are flagged instead of silently cut.

Measured, with limits stated

Accuracy is measured on labelled test transcripts, and the published results say plainly that these are synthetic, not real recordings.

Built so it can't run your channel behind your back

Everything here drafts or analyses. Acting on it is always your decision.

No publishing, replying or deleting

There is no code that writes to YouTube. Replies to comments are drafts you post yourself.

Read-only, if you connect at all

The optional YouTube connection asks only for read-only permission and refuses anything more.

Your files stay with you

The tools run locally on files you choose. Keys and sign-in tokens come from your environment and are never printed.

Approved edits only

An edit list can only be handed to a video engine after a person approves it, and any change after approval is caught.

Get started

Python 3.10 or newer. No other dependencies.

As command-line tools

pip install git+https://github.com/ICOFCUCAM/youtube-intelligence-engine
yt-title --title "Your title" --thumb "THUMB TEXT"

As Claude Code skills

/plugin marketplace add ICOFCUCAM/youtube-intelligence-engine
/plugin install youtube-intelligence

Then fill in templates/voice.md so every draft sounds like you.

For developers and partners

An independent intelligence layer you can call from your own studio or video engine.

Local HTTP API

Every tool as JSON over HTTP, with an OpenAPI 3.1 description. Loopback-only unless you set a token.

yt-serve
# GET /v1/openapi.json

Approved handoff

Approval records and a versioned production instruction (with a published JSON Schema) for a video engine to execute.

yt-handoff approve ...
yt-handoff instruction ...

Python library

Pure, deterministic functions underneath everything, plus an evaluation harness that scores them against labels.

from youtube_intelligence.editing import deadair
